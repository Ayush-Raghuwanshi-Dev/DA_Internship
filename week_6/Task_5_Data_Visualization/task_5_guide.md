- Color palettes curated for accessibility: Deep Navy `#1E3A8A`, Emerald Green `#10B981`, Crimson Red `#EF4444`, Amber `#F59E0B`.
- Every chart includes descriptive axis titles, labeled value annotations, and clear legends.
